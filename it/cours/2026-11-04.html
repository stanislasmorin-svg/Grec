Roma start very well. After eleven minutes, Soulé scores the first goal. Then Roma get a penalty. Malen shoots and scores again. At half-time Roma are two-nil up.

In the second half everything changes. Manchester get a penalty. Roma's goalkeeper saves it. But the referee has it taken again. This time Bruno Fernandes scores. Mbeumo hits the post twice. Then a Roma defender makes a mistake. Cunha scores two goals in a few minutes.
